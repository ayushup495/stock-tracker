About StockTrack

A stock register for shops and small factories — tracks what comes in, what goes out, and what's left, automatically. Works offline, installs like an app, and keeps every shop's data completely separate and private.

Multi-shop accounts, admin/viewer roles, low-stock alerts, item photos, and weekly usage reports by email.

Developed by Ayush Upadhyay

Clear old history

Removes transaction history older than the number of days you choose. Current stock totals stay accurate — only the detailed log is cleared. This cannot be undone.
